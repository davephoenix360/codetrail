## The shortlist (10 names, ranked loosely by brand-ability)

| # | Name | Style | Why it works | Concerns |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **CodeTrail** *(your pick)* | Compound | "Trail of commits" + "trail of progress." Memorable, intuitive, 2 syllables. | Common word — might be hard to grab `@codetrail` on X/Twitter. |
| 2 | **CommitPal** | Portmanteau | "Pal for your commits." Friendly, clear, easy mascot. | "Pal" is friendly but slightly kiddie. |
| 3 | **Shippr** | Made-up verb | "Ship + -pr" with doubled consonant for energy. Modern SaaS feel. | Looks like a typo at first glance. |
| 4 | **Streak** | Bold single word | Brute-force clarity. Owns the word. | Hard to get — `streak.com` is taken (HubSpot CRM). |
| 5 | **Buildy** | Friendly mascot | "Build + -y." Cute, approachable, easy to make a mascot for. | "Buildy" is already a Minecraft community manager. |
| 6 | **Commitful** | Portmanteau | "Commit + mindful." Suggests intentional shipping. | "Mindful" branding trend is aging. |
| 7 | **Pega** | Made-up brandable | "Peg your progress." Short, snappy, brandable. | Less obvious what it does. |
| 8 | **Streakify** | Portmanteau | Clear, brandable, distinct. | "Ify" suffix is getting crowded. |
| 9 | **CodeNymph** | Mythical / playful | Greek myth reference — "spirit of code." Pretty, distinctive. | May not vibe with the hype-man tone; nymphs are serene, not hype. |
| 10 | **Shipwright** | Old-English | "One who builds ships" → ships code. Strong, professional, distinctive. | Old-English might not feel modern/mobile-app. |

## Naming conventions I tried (for the algorithmically curious)

- **Portmanteaus** — `Commitful`, `Streakify`, `CodeCompanion`
- **Made-up words** — `Pega`, `Shippr`, `Yeb`
- **Friendly -y endings** — `Buildy`, `Committy`, `Shippy`
- **Mythical** — `CodeNymph`, `CommitPhoenix`
- **Single bold words** — `Streak`, `Ship`, `Push`
- **Personification** — `CommitPal`, `Codey`, `BuildBuddy`
- **`.dev` trends** — `codetrail.dev`, `commitpal.dev`

## My take

**CodeTrail works well** — it's intuitive, descriptive, has a real visual ("your trail of work"), and 2-syllable enough to say out loud. If you wanted a "more SaaS-y" vibe, **CommitPal** is the next best. If you wanted "more mascot-friendly" for marketing, **Buildy** or **Codey**.
